About Me

Hello! I am a PhD candidate in the Applied Mathematics and Statistics Department at Johns Hopkins University, where I have the great honor of being advised by Michael Dinitz. My research mainly focuses on theoretical computer science. Specifically, I'm doing Approximation and Online algorithms with machine learned predictions. Before my PhD, I earned an MS in Mathematics from the University of Oxford and a BS in Mathematics from the University of Liverpool.

I'm interested in using quantitative analysis to solve complex problems. Currently, I am seeking a summer internship in 2026.
